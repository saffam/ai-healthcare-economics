# Proposed AI training delivery methods

A blended learning approach is recommended.

## 1. Short foundational online learning

Useful for introductory concepts, common terminology, ethics and awareness. It should be accessible to different professional groups.

## 2. Interactive workshops

Useful for discussion, interpretation of AI outputs, uncertainty, professional responsibility and patient communication.

## 3. Clinical simulation

Useful for practising decisions in realistic scenarios without exposing patients to experimental training.

## 4. Supervised workplace learning

Where appropriate and authorised, professionals can learn the operation of a specific AI product under the supervision arrangements established by their organisation.

## 5. Refresher learning

Training should be reviewed when relevant software, clinical pathways, evidence or professional responsibilities change.

## 6. Multidisciplinary learning

Doctors, nurses, radiographers, technicians, pharmacists, informaticians and other relevant professionals should learn together where their responsibilities intersect.

## Design principle

Online completion should not be treated as proof of practical clinical competence. Practical users require assessment appropriate to their role and the risks of the technology.
