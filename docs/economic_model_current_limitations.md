# Current Economic Model Limitations

## Model status

The diabetic-retinopathy economic model is currently at the input-readiness stage.

The readiness assessment identifies 16 required parameters. None is currently marked as ready for calculation.

## Main limitations

1. The NHS-relevant screening population and period have not yet been finalised.
2. A suitable prevalence estimate for the specified population and disease threshold has not been verified.
3. AI and comparator sensitivity and specificity require source-level verification and confirmation of applicability.
4. The AI and comparator pathway costs have not been established.
5. Implementation, maintenance, training and monitoring costs remain unresolved.
6. Follow-up and false-positive pathway costs require further evidence.
7. The economic consequences of false-negative results require a defined clinical outcome framework.
8. The model time horizon requires explicit justification.

## Consequences

The model must not currently be used to claim that AI screening is cost-saving, cost-effective, cost-neutral or cost-increasing in NHS England.

The absence of a verified value must not be interpreted as a zero cost or zero effect.

## Permitted work at this stage

The project may document model structure, define equations, test calculations using artificial values, identify evidence requirements and conduct qualitative synthesis.

Any illustrative scenario must be clearly separated from empirical findings.

## Next requirement

Complete source-level evidence verification and update the parameter register before attempting substantive economic calculations.
