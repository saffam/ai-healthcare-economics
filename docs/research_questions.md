# Research questions

## Central research question

How can healthcare systems prepare existing and future healthcare professionals to work accurately, safely and effectively with artificial intelligence, while maintaining human clinical judgement and accountability?

## Supporting research questions

**RQ1 — AI literacy and professional competence**

What knowledge, skills and professional competencies do healthcare workers need to understand, interpret, evaluate and safely use AI-enabled healthcare technologies?

**RQ2 — Education and training design**

Which educational approaches, including foundational courses, specialist training, simulation and supervised practice, are supported by evidence for developing healthcare professionals' AI competence?

**RQ3 — Clinical accuracy and human–AI collaboration**

How can trained professionals identify incorrect, uncertain or biased AI outputs and combine AI recommendations with clinical findings and human judgement?

**RQ4 — Professional education and lifelong learning**

How should AI education be incorporated into undergraduate medical education, postgraduate training, other healthcare professional programmes and continuing professional development?

**RQ5 — Institutional readiness and governance**

What organisational, regulatory, ethical and operational conditions are necessary to support safe AI training, implementation, oversight and continuous improvement?

**RQ6 — Economic and service implications**

What are the costs, resource requirements and potential service implications of preparing the healthcare workforce for AI-assisted healthcare?

## Research emphasis

RQ1–RQ5 form the primary analytical focus. RQ6 provides supporting economic context.
