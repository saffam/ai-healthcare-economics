

# NHS economic evidence gaps

## Current position

The project has identified the NHS National Cost Collection as a potential source of relevant service costs. However, identifying a dataset does not establish that a compatible unit cost has been extracted or verified.

## Outstanding requirements

| Cost or parameter | Evidence required |
|---|---|
| Conventional screening | Cost matching the actual screening activity |
| AI assessment | Licence and per-assessment costs, where applicable |
| System integration | Deployment and interoperability expenditure |
| Training | Staff time and training expenditure |
| Oversight | Clinical and technical review resources |
| Maintenance | Recurring support and monitoring expenditure |
| Referral | Appropriate downstream pathway cost |
| Follow-up | Relevant surveillance and clinical activity costs |

## Interpretation

The model must remain not ready until its required inputs have been sourced, checked and documented.

The existence of NHS cost datasets does not mean every AI-specific cost can be derived from them. Supplier-specific and implementation-specific costs may require separate evidence.

**Current conclusion:** A definitive NHS-specific cost-effectiveness estimate is not supported by the current verified input set.
