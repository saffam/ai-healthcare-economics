# Maintaining healthcare AI competence

## Purpose

To prevent initial training from becoming outdated as AI products and clinical workflows change.

## Proposed maintenance cycle

1. Initial role-specific training.
2. Competency assessment.
3. Authorised use under the organisation's arrangements.
4. Periodic review of competence.
5. Refresher learning when relevant changes occur.
6. Review of incidents and lessons learned.
7. Reassessment when the risk or system changes justify it.

## Reassessment triggers

- Material AI software update.
- Change to intended use.
- Change in clinical pathway.
- New safety information.
- Repeated errors or inappropriate use.
- Extended period without using the system.
- Changes in relevant professional or regulatory guidance.

## Responsibility

The healthcare organisation should define who maintains training records, who provides refresher education and who authorises use of the relevant system.

## Principle

Competence is not permanent merely because a course was completed once. Maintenance should be proportionate to the clinical risk and rate of change.
