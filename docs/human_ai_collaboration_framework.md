# Human–AI collaboration framework

## Purpose

This framework explains how clinical professionals, AI systems and healthcare organisations contribute to safe AI-assisted decisions.

## Three interacting components

### 1. Technical system

The AI system should have:
- Appropriate clinical validation.
- Clearly defined intended use.
- Known limitations and uncertainty.
- Relevant performance information.
- Monitoring for performance changes.
- Processes for reporting and investigating problems.

### 2. Trained professional

The professional should be able to:
- Understand the system's intended use.
- Interpret its output in context.
- Recognise uncertainty and potential error.
- Compare recommendations with clinical information.
- Challenge, reject or escalate outputs when appropriate.
- Explain the role of AI to patients.
- Retain appropriate professional responsibility.

### 3. Supporting organisation

The organisation should provide:
- Role-specific training.
- Protected learning time.
- Access to technical and clinical support.
- Clear accountability and escalation procedures.
- Appropriate staffing and workflow design.
- Incident reporting and learning.
- Regular review of system performance.

## Core principle

Safe AI-assisted healthcare is a property of the combined system. It cannot be attributed to algorithm accuracy, staff training or organisational policy in isolation.
