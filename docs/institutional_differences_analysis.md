# Institutional Differences in Healthcare AI Adoption

## 1. Purpose

This document examines why the same healthcare AI technology may produce different outcomes across healthcare organisations.

## 2. Organisational readiness

Healthcare organisations differ in:

- Digital infrastructure.
- Data quality.
- IT interoperability.
- Workforce capacity.
- Leadership.
- Procurement arrangements.
- Financial resources.
- Clinical governance.
- Technical support.

These differences can influence implementation and performance.

## 3. Infrastructure

An AI system may require compatible imaging equipment, secure data transfer, reliable computing infrastructure and integration with existing clinical systems.

Organisations with limited infrastructure may face additional implementation expenditure.

## 4. Workforce capacity

Organisations may differ in the availability of trained professionals and technical staff.

Training and implementation requirements can therefore vary.

## 5. Organisational culture

Staff engagement, leadership support, communication and confidence in technology may influence implementation.

## 6. Differences between healthcare systems

International studies may be conducted in healthcare systems with different:

- Wage levels.
- Procurement costs.
- Reimbursement arrangements.
- Screening pathways.
- Patient populations.
- Regulatory requirements.
- Healthcare financing arrangements.

Their economic results cannot automatically be transferred to the NHS.

## 7. Evidence

Powis et al. (2026) synthesise evidence on barriers and facilitators to healthcare AI implementation.

Their framework identifies multiple domains involving design, organisational culture, deployment, quality, safety and equity.

## 8. Implications for the economic model

The model should distinguish system-level inputs from organisation-specific implementation requirements.

Where possible, implementation costs should be examined under alternative organisational scenarios.

Unsupported assumptions about identical implementation conditions should be avoided.

## 9. Conclusion

Institutional context is an important determinant of healthcare AI implementation and economic value.

The same technology may produce different results because organisations differ in their resources, workforce, infrastructure and healthcare pathways.
