
# Economic Benefits Framework for Healthcare AI

## 1. Purpose

This framework identifies potential economic, operational and health-related benefits associated with AI adoption in healthcare.

Potential benefits will be treated as research questions rather than assumed outcomes.

## 2. Operational Benefits

Potential areas of investigation include:

- Reduced administrative workload.
- Improved workflow organisation.
- Faster information processing.
- Improved resource scheduling.
- Reduced duplication of tasks.

## 3. Workforce and Productivity Benefits

Potential areas of investigation include:

- Time released for other professional activities.
- Changes in staff workload.
- Improved task allocation.
- Support for professional decision-making.
- Changes in service capacity.

Time saved will not automatically be interpreted as increased productivity.

## 4. Clinical and Health-Related Benefits

Potential areas of investigation include:

- Diagnostic performance.
- Decision-support performance.
- Patient safety.
- Treatment timeliness.
- Patient outcomes.
- Quality of care.

Technical accuracy will be distinguished from improvements in actual patient outcomes.

## 5. Resource Allocation Benefits

Potential areas of investigation include:

- Improved allocation of healthcare resources.
- Reduced unnecessary service utilisation.
- Improved planning and forecasting.
- Better use of existing infrastructure.

## 6. Distribution of Benefits

The research will consider:

- Benefits to healthcare providers.
- Benefits to healthcare professionals.
- Benefits to patients.
- Benefits to healthcare payers.
- Wider public health benefits.

## 7. Measurement Requirements

Each claimed benefit should be assessed according to:

- Its definition.
- The measurement method.
- The relevant comparator.
- The size of the reported effect.
- The time period.
- The uncertainty.
- The relevance to the healthcare setting.

## 8. Reporting Principle

The research will distinguish between projected, expected and observed benefits.

Potential benefits will not be treated as realised economic savings without supporting evidence.
