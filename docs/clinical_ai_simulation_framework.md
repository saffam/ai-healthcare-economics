# Clinical AI simulation framework

## Purpose

To help healthcare professionals practise the interpretation and challenge of AI outputs in a controlled learning environment.

## Scenario categories

1. Correct AI recommendation with supporting clinical information.
2. Incorrect positive recommendation.
3. Incorrect negative recommendation.
4. AI output that conflicts with clinical findings.
5. Uncertain or low-confidence output.
6. Poor-quality or ungradable image.
7. Potentially biased performance across patient groups.
8. System unavailable or workflow interrupted.
9. AI output outside the system's intended use.
10. Patient asks how AI influenced a clinical decision.

## Assessment dimensions

- Recognition of the system's intended use.
- Recognition of limitations and uncertainty.
- Appropriate use of other clinical information.
- Ability to challenge an AI recommendation.
- Correct escalation and human review.
- Communication and documentation.
- Appropriate professional judgement.

## Safety boundary

These scenarios are educational simulations. They must not be represented as validated clinical examinations or proof of real-world diagnostic improvement.
