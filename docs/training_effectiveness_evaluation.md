# Evaluation of healthcare AI training effectiveness

## Evaluation framework

The project proposes evaluating training at four progressively more demanding levels.

### Level 1 — Learner reaction

Questions:
- Was the course relevant?
- Was the material understandable?
- Did participants consider it useful?

Evidence: Course feedback.

### Level 2 — Knowledge and competence

Questions:
- Can learners explain AI limitations?
- Can they interpret diagnostic performance?
- Can they recognise incorrect AI outputs?

Evidence: Knowledge tests and assessed simulations.

### Level 3 — Workplace behaviour

Questions:
- Do trained professionals apply appropriate checking procedures?
- Do they challenge and escalate outputs when required?
- Do they follow approved governance procedures?

Evidence: Properly designed workplace evaluations and audits.

### Level 4 — Organisational and patient outcomes

Questions:
- Has implementation improved clinical processes?
- Has safety or decision quality changed?
- Are patient outcomes improved?

Evidence: Appropriately designed organisational and clinical evaluations.

## Important limitation

Improvement at one level does not establish improvement at the next. High course satisfaction does not prove competence, and improved simulation scores do not prove better patient outcomes.

## Project decision

The four levels are an evaluation structure for the proposed framework. The project will not claim that a particular training programme has achieved patient benefit unless suitable published evidence demonstrates it.
