# Economic Outcome Definitions

## 1. Total cost

The sum of relevant costs incurred by a screening pathway over the specified time horizon and from the specified perspective.

## 2. Incremental cost

The difference between the total cost of the AI-assisted pathway and the total cost of the comparator pathway.

Incremental cost = AI pathway cost minus comparator pathway cost.

## 3. Cost saving

A reduction in expenditure relative to the comparator.

A reduction in staff time is not automatically a cash saving.

## 4. Released capacity

Staff time or service capacity made available for other activities.

Released capacity should be reported separately from cash-releasing savings.

## 5. Sensitivity

The proportion of people with the target condition correctly identified by the test.

## 6. Specificity

The proportion of people without the target condition correctly classified as negative.

## 7. Positive predictive value

The proportion of positive results that are true positives.

## 8. Negative predictive value

The proportion of negative results that are true negatives.

Predictive values depend on disease prevalence in the screened population.

## 9. Incremental cost-effectiveness ratio

Where suitable outcome data are available:

ICER = incremental cost divided by incremental health outcome.

## 10. Quality-adjusted life-year

A measure combining length of life and health-related quality of life.

A QALY analysis requires appropriate outcome evidence and utility estimates. It cannot be derived from diagnostic accuracy alone.

## 11. Budget impact

The expected financial effect of adopting a technology within a defined budget-holder population and period.

Budget impact is distinct from cost-effectiveness.

## 12. Uncertainty

Uncertainty in model inputs, model structure, clinical evidence, costs and transferability.

It should be examined using appropriate sensitivity and scenario analyses.
