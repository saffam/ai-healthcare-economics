# Economic Evidence Gap Priorities

## Priority 1 — Define the model population

Specify the screening population, geographical scope, screening period and denominator. Without a defined population, expected case counts cannot be interpreted.

## Priority 2 — Establish diagnostic performance

Verify AI and comparator sensitivity and specificity for comparable disease thresholds, populations and screening conditions.

## Priority 3 — Map the care pathway

Define the steps following a positive, negative, ungradable or uncertain result. Identify which activities are included in each pathway.

## Priority 4 — Establish NHS costs

Identify applicable NHS costs for screening, grading, referral, follow-up, implementation, training, maintenance and monitoring.

## Priority 5 — Define consequences of diagnostic errors

Specify the clinical consequences and downstream resource use associated with false-positive and false-negative results. Do not assume these are equivalent or immediate.

## Priority 6 — Justify the time horizon

Choose a horizon that reflects the relevant screening and disease progression consequences. Document the rationale.

## Priority 7 — Assess uncertainty

Identify uncertainty in the evidence and model structure. Use scenario or sensitivity analysis only where ranges and assumptions can be justified.

## Current decision

Do not calculate a real-world NHS cost-effectiveness result until the necessary inputs and outcomes are supported by evidence.
