# Institutional Context and Differences Framework

## 1. Purpose

This framework examines why the economic, operational and clinical effects of healthcare AI may differ between healthcare organisations and systems.

The research recognises that the same technology may produce different results depending on the environment in which it is introduced. Technical performance alone cannot establish whether an AI system will create economic or clinical value in every setting.

## 2. Institutional Factors

The research will consider the following factors.

### 2.1 Financial resources

Organisations may differ in their ability to fund:

- Initial acquisition or development.
- Digital infrastructure.
- Data preparation and management.
- Staff training.
- Integration with existing systems.
- Ongoing maintenance and monitoring.

Financial constraints may affect implementation quality and the distribution of potential benefits.

### 2.2 Digital infrastructure

Relevant differences may include:

- Availability and reliability of digital systems.
- Interoperability between information systems.
- Data quality and accessibility.
- Cybersecurity arrangements.
- Technical support and maintenance capacity.

### 2.3 Workforce capacity

The research will examine:

- Availability of appropriately trained staff.
- Time available for training and implementation.
- Digital and analytical skills.
- Clinical workload.
- Availability of technical and managerial support.

### 2.4 Leadership and organisational readiness

Relevant factors may include:

- Senior leadership support.
- Clear implementation responsibilities.
- Staff involvement in adoption decisions.
- Organisational learning and feedback.
- Procedures for identifying and responding to problems.

### 2.5 Clinical workflows

The analysis will consider whether AI systems:

- Fit existing clinical processes.
- Require additional administrative work.
- Duplicate existing tasks.
- Change referral or decision-making pathways.
- Affect communication between professional groups.

### 2.6 Healthcare system arrangements

The research will consider differences in:

- Funding and payment arrangements.
- Procurement and commissioning.
- Regulatory and governance requirements.
- Service organisation.
- Access to specialist services.
- Data governance and information-sharing arrangements.

## 3. NHS England as the Initial Focus

NHS England will provide the initial geographical and institutional focus for the research.

International evidence may be included where it offers useful comparisons or relevant insights. However, differences in healthcare financing, regulation, workforce organisation and service delivery must be considered before transferring findings to the NHS context.

## 4. Transferability of Evidence

For each relevant study, the research will record:

- Country and healthcare system.
- Type of healthcare organisation.
- Clinical setting.
- AI application.
- Implementation conditions.
- Relevant differences from the NHS England context.
- Limitations affecting transferability.

The project will distinguish between evidence that is directly relevant to the NHS and evidence that provides indirect or comparative insight.

## 5. Economic Implications

Institutional differences may affect:

- Initial and ongoing implementation costs.
- The scale of achievable productivity improvements.
- Staff time and training requirements.
- Service capacity and patient access.
- The distribution of costs and benefits.
- The sustainability of AI adoption.

The research will consider whether economic value depends on organisational conditions rather than assuming that the technology itself determines the outcome.

## 6. Analytical Principles

The research will:

1. Avoid assuming that all healthcare organisations have equivalent resources.
2. Distinguish technology performance from implementation performance.
3. Consider local workflows and organisational capacity.
4. Examine whether benefits observed in one setting are transferable.
5. Recognise that implementation costs may differ substantially.
6. Consider smaller and resource-constrained organisations where evidence is available.

## 7. Limitations

Institutional characteristics are not always reported consistently in published research. It may therefore be difficult to isolate the contribution of individual organisational factors.

The project will describe these limitations and avoid unsupported explanations for differences between organisations.

## 8. Relationship to the Research Questions

This framework primarily informs RQ5 and contributes to RQ1, RQ2, RQ3 and RQ6.
