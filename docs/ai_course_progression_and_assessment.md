# AI course progression and assessment plan

## Stage 1 — Baseline assessment

Establish learners' existing understanding of AI, relevant clinical tasks and confidence.

The baseline should distinguish self-reported confidence from demonstrated knowledge.

## Stage 2 — Foundational education

Provide common learning on AI concepts, limitations, uncertainty, privacy, bias and professional responsibilities.

Assessment: knowledge test and interpretation questions.

## Stage 3 — Role-specific learning

Teach the operation and limitations of the AI application relevant to the learner's professional role.

Assessment: application-specific case assessment.

## Stage 4 — Simulation

Present realistic scenarios, including incorrect, uncertain and conflicting AI outputs.

Assessment: observed ability to recognise errors, use clinical context and escalate appropriately.

## Stage 5 — Supervised application

Where the organisation has authorised the technology and the professional is appropriately supervised, provide structured workplace learning.

Assessment: documented supervisor feedback and approved competency evidence.

## Stage 6 — Continuing development

Provide refresher learning following material system changes, new evidence or identified performance concerns.

Assessment: periodic reassessment proportionate to risk.

## Completion principle

Course attendance is not equivalent to clinical competence. A professional should only use a system within the permissions, training and supervision arrangements established by their organisation.
