# Future evaluation protocol: healthcare AI training

## Research purpose

To evaluate whether a role-specific AI training programme improves healthcare professionals' ability to interpret and challenge AI outputs.

## Proposed research questions

1. Does training improve knowledge of AI limitations?
2. Does it improve recognition of incorrect or uncertain outputs?
3. Does it improve appropriate escalation?
4. Are improvements retained over time?
5. Do effects differ by profession or previous experience?

## Possible design

A prospective educational evaluation using pre-training, post-training and follow-up assessments.

A comparison between alternative teaching approaches could be considered if feasible.

## Participants

Any future participants would be healthcare professionals or students recruited under an approved research protocol.

No participants are recruited as part of the present project.

## Outcomes

Primary outcome: performance on a predefined and appropriately validated assessment of AI-output interpretation.

Secondary outcomes: knowledge, confidence calibration, error recognition, escalation decisions and retention.

## Analysis

The future study should prespecify outcomes, measurement instruments, sample-size justification and an appropriate statistical analysis plan.

## Ethics and governance

Any real-world evaluation involving human participants must obtain the appropriate ethics, institutional and data-governance approvals before recruitment or data collection.

## Status

Proposal only. This is not an approved clinical or educational research study.
