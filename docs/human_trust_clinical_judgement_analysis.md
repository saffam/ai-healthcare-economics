# Human Trust and Clinical Judgement in AI-Assisted Healthcare

## 1. Purpose

This document examines the relationship between human trust, clinical judgement and AI-assisted healthcare decisions.

## 2. Trust in healthcare AI

Healthcare professionals need to understand the capabilities and limitations of AI systems before relying on their outputs.

Trust should be based on evidence of appropriate performance, validation, reliability and suitability for the clinical context.

## 3. Factors influencing trust

Relevant factors include:

- Transparency.
- Explainability.
- Reliability.
- External validation.
- Usability.
- Training.
- Professional autonomy.
- Patient safety.
- Organisational governance.
- Accountability.

## 4. Appropriate versus excessive trust

Insufficient trust may lead professionals to disregard useful decision support.

Excessive trust may lead to inappropriate reliance on system outputs.

The objective should therefore be calibrated trust: confidence that reflects demonstrated performance and known limitations.

## 5. Clinical judgement

AI outputs should be interpreted within the relevant clinical context.

Professionals may need to consider patient history, image quality, uncertainty, conflicting evidence and conditions outside the system's intended use.

## 6. Evidence

Tun et al. (2025) conducted a systematic review of healthcare workers' trust in AI-based clinical decision support systems.

The review identifies transparency, training, usability, reliability, credibility, ethics, human-centred design and professional autonomy as relevant themes.

## 7. Economic implications

Trust can influence:

- Adoption.
- Staff training requirements.
- System utilisation.
- Human review.
- Workflow integration.
- Implementation success.

However, the economic impact of trust should not be assigned a monetary value without an appropriate measurement method.

## 8. Patient trust

Patient understanding and acceptance may influence participation and the perceived legitimacy of AI-supported care.

Patients should receive appropriate information about the role of AI and the involvement of healthcare professionals.

## 9. Conclusion

Trust is an important human and organisational dimension of healthcare AI.

Safe adoption requires appropriate validation, transparency, professional oversight and clear accountability rather than unquestioning acceptance of automated outputs.
