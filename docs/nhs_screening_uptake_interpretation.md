

# Interpretation of the NHS diabetic eye screening uptake KPI

## Reported measure

The Q4 2023/24 national KPI for routine digital screening was 80.8%.

The reported numerator was 2,520,323 and the denominator was 3,119,795.

The KPI describes the proportion of people offered routine digital screening who attended an event at which images were captured. It is an annual rolling measure reported for the quarter.

## Appropriate interpretation

The KPI is useful for describing screening participation and establishing a baseline service context.

It may help identify the importance of attendance when considering the potential population-level consequences of a screening intervention.

## Inappropriate interpretation

The KPI does not show that AI increased attendance, improved diagnostic accuracy or reduced costs.

It must not be described as an AI intervention outcome.

## Modelling restriction

Do not use the 80.8% figure as a universal attendance assumption for every year, population or local service. Any model using it must identify the reporting period and explain why it is relevant to the modelled population.
