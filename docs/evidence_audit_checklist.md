

# Evidence audit checklist

Use this checklist before promoting the manuscript from working draft to final research report.

## A. References

- [ ] Full author lists checked.
- [ ] Article titles checked against original publications.
- [ ] Journal, year, volume and article numbers checked.
- [ ] DOI links checked.
- [ ] Incomplete references replaced with verified citations.

## B. Clinical evidence

- [ ] Study populations recorded.
- [ ] Study designs recorded.
- [ ] Diagnostic thresholds recorded.
- [ ] Sensitivity and specificity extracted separately.
- [ ] Confidence intervals recorded.
- [ ] Imaging and dilation conditions recorded.
- [ ] Reference standards recorded.
- [ ] Limitations and risk of bias assessed.

## C. NHS pathway evidence

- [ ] Applicable national standards identified.
- [ ] Pathway requirements checked.
- [ ] Screening KPI definitions recorded.
- [ ] Numerators and denominators checked.
- [ ] Reporting periods recorded.
- [ ] Descriptive statistics not presented as causal effects.

## D. Economic evidence

- [ ] Economic perspective defined.
- [ ] Time horizon justified.
- [ ] Comparator clearly specified.
- [ ] Cost definitions recorded.
- [ ] Price years recorded.
- [ ] AI-specific costs investigated.
- [ ] Missing values retained as unresolved.
- [ ] Model inputs approved individually.

## E. Reproducibility and reporting

- [ ] Python validation tests pass.
- [ ] Model readiness report regenerated.
- [ ] Figures have source notes.
- [ ] Tables have definitions and units.
- [ ] References are cited in the manuscript.
- [ ] Limitations are accurately reported.
- [ ] No unsupported economic conclusions remain.

## Final release condition

The manuscript may be described as a completed independent evidence review only when its methods, source selection, extraction and synthesis are documented sufficiently to support that description.

It may report a numerical NHS economic result only when the relevant inputs and calculations are verified.

A passing software test alone does not satisfy these conditions.
