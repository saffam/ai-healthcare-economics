# Continuing professional development for healthcare AI

## Rationale

AI competence should not be treated as a one-time qualification. Systems, clinical pathways, evidence and professional responsibilities can change.

## Proposed CPD structure

- Initial foundational AI learning.
- Role-specific training before use of relevant clinical AI systems.
- Periodic refresher learning.
- Additional training after material system changes.
- Review of incidents and lessons learned.
- Access to updated guidance and learning resources.
- Opportunities for multidisciplinary discussion.

## Triggers for additional training

1. Introduction of a new AI product.
2. Significant software or model update.
3. Changes to intended use.
4. Changes to clinical workflow.
5. Identification of recurring errors.
6. New evidence affecting safe use.
7. Changes in relevant policy or professional guidance.

## Evaluation

CPD participation should be recorded separately from demonstrated competence. Attendance alone should not be treated as evidence that an individual can safely interpret AI outputs.
