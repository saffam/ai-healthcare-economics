# Research aim and objectives

## Main aim

To examine how healthcare organisations can prepare existing and future healthcare professionals to use artificial intelligence accurately, safely and effectively, while preserving clinical judgement, accountability and patient-centred care.

## Objectives

1. Examine the AI literacy and clinical competencies required across healthcare professions.
2. Assess evidence on the effectiveness and limitations of AI education and training programmes.
3. Develop a tiered training framework for existing healthcare staff, students and specialist AI leaders.
4. Examine how simulation, supervised practice and competency assessment could prepare staff for incorrect or uncertain AI outputs.
5. Investigate how human oversight, professional responsibility and patient communication should operate in AI-assisted decisions.
6. Identify institutional conditions needed to support training, including protected learning time, leadership, infrastructure and continuing professional development.
7. Examine how training effectiveness can be evaluated, from knowledge acquisition to workplace behaviour, organisational change and patient outcomes.
8. Consider the economic implications of workforce preparation as a supporting rather than dominant research objective.

## Research boundary

This is independent secondary research. It does not involve recruiting healthcare workers or patients, conducting clinical experiments, accessing patient records or claiming formal NHS approval.
