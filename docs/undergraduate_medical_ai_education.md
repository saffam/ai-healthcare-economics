# AI education in undergraduate medical education

## Purpose

Prepare future doctors to enter a healthcare environment in which AI may support diagnosis, screening, administration and clinical decision-making.

## Proposed curriculum areas

### Early medical education

- Basic AI literacy.
- Data and algorithm fundamentals.
- Healthcare applications.
- Ethics, privacy and fairness.

### Clinical years

- Interpretation of AI-supported clinical information.
- Diagnostic accuracy and uncertainty.
- Human judgement and automation bias.
- Patient communication.
- Case-based simulation.

### Final-year preparation

- AI use within professional scope.
- Clinical responsibility and escalation.
- Safe workflow integration.
- Critical appraisal of clinical AI evidence.

## Educational principle

AI education should complement, not replace, foundational clinical knowledge, communication skills, clinical reasoning or supervised clinical experience.

## Accreditation boundary

This is a proposed curriculum framework. It does not claim approval by the GMC, medical schools or any professional regulator.
