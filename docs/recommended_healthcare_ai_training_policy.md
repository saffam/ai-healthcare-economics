# Recommended approach to healthcare AI training

## Recommendation 1: Establish foundational AI literacy

Relevant healthcare workers should have access to accessible education covering AI concepts, limitations, uncertainty, bias, privacy and professional responsibility.

## Recommendation 2: Make training role-specific

Professionals who interpret or act on clinical AI outputs should receive training specific to the relevant technology and clinical workflow.

## Recommendation 3: Assess competence, not just attendance

Practical users should demonstrate appropriate interpretation, error recognition and escalation skills.

## Recommendation 4: Include simulation

Training should include realistic cases in which AI outputs are incorrect, uncertain, incomplete or inconsistent with other clinical information.

## Recommendation 5: Preserve clinical reasoning

AI education should strengthen, rather than replace, the development of clinical knowledge and independent professional judgement.

## Recommendation 6: Provide continuing professional development

Training should be reviewed when AI systems, intended uses, clinical pathways or safety information change.

## Recommendation 7: Prepare organisations as well as individuals

Healthcare providers should establish protected learning time, clinical leadership, technical support, monitoring and clear accountability.

## Recommendation 8: Evaluate outcomes progressively

Training should be evaluated from learner knowledge to practical competence, workplace behaviour and, where suitable evidence exists, organisational and patient outcomes.

## Recommendation 9: Integrate AI into future professional education

Medical, nursing and allied health education should introduce AI literacy and critical evaluation in ways appropriate to each profession and stage of learning.

## Recommendation 10: Treat economic considerations as supporting evidence

Training and implementation costs should be considered, but financial savings should not override safety, competence or patient-centred care.

## Status

These are recommendations developed through independent secondary research. They are not official NHS policy or a substitute for professional or regulatory requirements.
