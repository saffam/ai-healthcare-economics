

# Rules for using clinical diagnostic estimates

1. Record the exact target condition and diagnostic threshold.
2. Record sensitivity and specificity separately.
3. Record confidence intervals, not only point estimates.
4. Record whether images were dilated or undilated.
5. Record the camera and imaging protocol where available.
6. Record the reference standard.
7. Distinguish participant counts from image counts.
8. Record ungradable images and exclusions where reported.
9. Do not combine estimates from incompatible populations or thresholds.
10. Do not treat pooled international estimates as NHS-specific performance.
11. Do not use abstract-only numerical estimates as final economic model inputs.
12. Record the source and page, table or figure supporting each extracted value.

A numerical estimate may be included in the narrative literature review before it is approved for modelling. These are separate decisions.
