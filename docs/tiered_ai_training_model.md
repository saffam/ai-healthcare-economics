# Tiered AI training model for healthcare

## Level 1 — Foundational AI literacy

**Audience:** Relevant healthcare staff, including professionals who may encounter AI outputs but do not directly operate clinical AI systems.

**Purpose:** Develop basic understanding and awareness.

Core learning:
- What AI is and common healthcare applications.
- Intended use and limitations.
- Basic risks, bias and uncertainty.
- Patient privacy and information governance.
- Appropriate escalation and professional responsibility.

**Completion evidence:** Knowledge assessment and role-relevant scenario questions.

## Level 2 — Role-specific clinical AI competence

**Audience:** Professionals who use, interpret, verify or act upon AI outputs.

**Purpose:** Develop safe practical competence.

Core learning:
- Interpreting AI outputs.
- Understanding diagnostic performance.
- Recognising false positives and false negatives.
- Handling uncertain or ungradable results.
- Challenging and escalating AI recommendations.
- Communicating AI-assisted decisions.
- Applying human review procedures.

**Completion evidence:** Observed case-based simulation and competency assessment.

## Level 3 — Specialist AI implementation and oversight

**Audience:** Designated clinical AI leads, clinical informaticians, safety specialists, educators and implementation leaders.

**Purpose:** Support evaluation and governance of AI systems.

Core learning:
- Clinical validation and evaluation.
- Data quality and bias.
- Workflow and implementation assessment.
- Monitoring and incident investigation.
- Governance and accountability.
- Training design and assessment.
- Organisational change and continuous improvement.

**Completion evidence:** Applied project, portfolio or supervised assessment.

## Important distinction

These are proposed educational levels for the research framework, not existing NHS qualifications or accredited courses. Completion of a level does not confer permission to practise outside a professional's existing scope.
