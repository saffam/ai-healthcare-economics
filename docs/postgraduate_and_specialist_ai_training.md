# Postgraduate and specialist AI training

## Purpose

Develop advanced competencies for doctors and other healthcare professionals whose clinical roles require regular interaction with AI-enabled technologies.

## Proposed elements

1. Specialty-specific clinical AI applications.
2. Critical evaluation of AI recommendations.
3. AI performance and diagnostic uncertainty.
4. Clinical risk and escalation.
5. Human oversight and accountability.
6. AI-supported multidisciplinary decision-making.
7. Communication with patients about AI.
8. Supervised use of approved systems.
9. Evaluation of changes in clinical workflow.
10. Continuing professional development.

## Specialty adaptation

Training should use examples relevant to the clinical specialty. For example:

- Radiology: image interpretation and AI-generated findings.
- Ophthalmology: screening, image quality and referral decisions.
- Pathology: digital slides and algorithm-supported classification.
- General practice: clinical decision support and documentation.
- Pharmacy: medication-related decision support.

## Principle

Specialist education should address the technology and the clinical workflow in which it is used. A generic AI course cannot replace product-specific and specialty-specific training.
