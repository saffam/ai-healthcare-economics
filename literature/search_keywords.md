## Search Development

Keywords will be combined using Boolean operators:

AND = narrows a search by requiring both concepts.

OR = broadens a search by accepting either concept.

Example:

("artificial intelligence" OR "machine learning")
AND
(healthcare OR hospital)
AND
(economic evaluation OR cost-effectiveness)
