
# Literature Screening Criteria

## Purpose

These criteria establish a consistent approach to selecting research and institutional publications for the project.

## Inclusion Criteria

A source may be included if it:

1. Examines artificial intelligence in a healthcare context.
2. Addresses at least one research question.
3. Has identifiable authors or a credible institutional publisher.
4. Provides a publication date or identifiable version.
5. Describes its evidence, methodology or policy basis sufficiently for assessment.
6. Provides relevant findings or information.
7. Has an accessible abstract, report or full text sufficient for the intended use.

## Exclusion Criteria

A source will generally be excluded if it:

1. Is primarily promotional material without independent supporting evidence.
2. Has unverifiable authorship or provenance.
3. Does not relate meaningfully to the research questions.
4. Makes claims without an identifiable evidence basis.
5. Contains data whose proposed use is prohibited or unclear.
6. Cannot be assessed sufficiently for the purpose for which it is being considered.

## Screening Stages

Stage 1: Review title and abstract or executive summary.

Stage 2: Review the full text where accessible.

Stage 3: Assess relevance, credibility, methodology and limitations.

Stage 4: Record the inclusion or exclusion decision and reason.

## Important Principle

Studies reporting negative, neutral or inconclusive findings will not be excluded because of their results.

The selection process will aim to minimise confirmation bias and represent the available evidence fairly.
