
# Literature Search Strategy

## Purpose

The literature search will identify academic and institutional evidence relevant to the economic, human, organisational and policy implications of artificial intelligence in healthcare.

## Search Platforms

1. PubMed
2. Europe PMC
3. Google Scholar
4. NICE
5. World Health Organization
6. NHS England
7. UK government publications
8. Relevant health economics journals and institutional repositories

## Main Search Themes

### Theme 1: Economic Evaluation
- Artificial intelligence AND healthcare economics
- Artificial intelligence AND cost-effectiveness
- AI AND health technology assessment
- AI AND healthcare productivity

### Theme 2: Human Trust and Decision-Making
- AI AND automation bias AND healthcare
- Human-AI collaboration AND clinical decision-making
- Trust AND clinical decision support
- AI AND professional autonomy

### Theme 3: Institutional Implementation
- AI adoption AND hospitals
- Healthcare AI AND organisational readiness
- AI implementation AND health systems
- AI AND institutional capacity

### Theme 4: Workforce
- AI AND healthcare workforce
- Healthcare automation AND labour productivity
- AI AND task substitution
- AI AND professional roles

### Theme 5: Policy and Governance
- AI AND healthcare reimbursement
- AI AND health policy
- AI AND accountability
- AI AND healthcare regulation

## Search Documentation

For every search, record:

- Database or website.
- Date of search.
- Exact search terms.
- Filters used.
- Number of results where available.
- Relevant records identified.

## Search Principles

Searches will be conducted transparently and consistently.

Relevant older foundational research will not be excluded solely because of publication date. Recent evidence will be considered particularly important for current AI technologies and policy developments.

The search strategy may be refined where necessary, with changes documented.
