# NHS Diabetic Eye Screening Coverage: Data Provenance

## Dataset

File: `data/processed/nhs_screening_coverage.csv`

## Source

Department of Health and Social Care (2025), Health Trends and Variation in England, 2025, figure 5.17.

## Geography

England.

## Period

2015 to 2024.

## Measure

The published figure describes the percentage of people offered a routine diabetic eye screening appointment who attended and completed a routine digital screening event where images were captured.

## Precision

The values entered in the CSV are approximate rounded values read from the published figure. They are not exact counts or provider-level observations.

## Permitted use

The dataset may be used for:

- A descriptive trend chart.
- Contextual discussion of screening programme coverage.
- Identifying the disruption and subsequent recovery around the COVID-19 period.

## Prohibited interpretation

The dataset must not be used to claim:

- That AI caused changes in screening coverage.
- That coverage measures diagnostic accuracy.
- That coverage is equivalent to the proportion of all people with diabetes who receive screening.
- That the values represent exact administrative counts.

## Limitations

The values are approximate and should be replaced with exact official data if a suitable downloadable dataset is obtained and checked.

## Reproducibility

The original source, figure number, geography, period and measurement definition are recorded here so another researcher can trace the dataset.
