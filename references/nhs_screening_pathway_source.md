

# NHS diabetic eye screening pathway requirements

**Issuing organisation:** NHS England

**Official document:** Diabetic eye screening pathway requirements

**Source:** https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/diabetic-eye-screening-pathway-requirements-specification/diabetic-eye-screening-pathway-requirements-specification

**Latest update identified:** 16 April 2025

## Pathway relevance

The document describes what should happen at each stage of the NHS Diabetic Eye Screening Programme pathway.

## Use in this research

Use it to establish the service pathway before evaluating the potential consequences of AI. The analysis should consider invitation, attendance, image capture, grading or assessment, communication of results, referral, surveillance and follow-up where applicable.

## Important distinction

A pathway description is not a measurement of the economic impact of AI. It provides the service context against which an intervention could be evaluated.
