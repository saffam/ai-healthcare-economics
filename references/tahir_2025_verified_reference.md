## Study characteristics

The review included 25 observational studies published between January 2015 and December 2024, with 613,690 images reported across the included studies. It compared AI and manual screening performance, including dilated and undilated imaging.

## Important interpretation

The pooled estimates differ according to whether eyes were dilated. The results must therefore be reported with their imaging conditions and confidence intervals. The review's pooled diagnostic estimates are not direct estimates of NHS service effectiveness or cost-effectiveness.

## Use in this project

Use this source for the comparative diagnostic evidence review. Do not use its pooled estimates as NHS model inputs without checking the included study populations, reference standards, screening thresholds and relevance to the intended NHS pathway.
