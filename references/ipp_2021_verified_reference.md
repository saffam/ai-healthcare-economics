## Study relevance

This study evaluates an AI system for autonomous detection of referable and vision-threatening diabetic retinopathy. It is relevant to the distinction between autonomous screening and AI-assisted screening.

## Interpretation limitations

The study evaluates a particular system and study configuration. Its results must not be generalised automatically to other AI products, populations, cameras, clinical pathways or NHS England.
