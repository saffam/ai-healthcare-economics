

# NHS National Cost Collection: source record

**Publisher:** NHS England

**Main source:** https://www.england.nhs.uk/costing-in-the-nhs/national-cost-collection/

**2024/25 publication:** https://www.england.nhs.uk/publication/2024-25-national-cost-collection-data-publication/

**Publication date:** 6 November 2025

## Description

The National Cost Collection includes aggregated costs for defined NHS services and patient-level costing information. NHS providers submit cost information annually.

## Potential research use

The collection may help identify relevant NHS service costs, depending on whether the activity and cost category needed for the diabetic eye screening model are available and sufficiently comparable.

## Limitations

A national average cost for a service is not automatically the marginal cost of an additional screening event, referral or AI assessment.

A cost category must match the activity being modelled. The collection does not automatically provide the acquisition, licence, integration, maintenance or training costs of a specific AI product.
