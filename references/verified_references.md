## 4. Referencing and verification notes

1. DOI links should be used wherever a DOI is available.
2. The year of the journal issue should be used for journal references. Where an article was published online in an earlier year, this may be explained in the text if relevant.
3. Institutional web guidance should be cited using its confirmed publication or update date where available. The access date should be recorded for live guidance.
4. The abbreviated Wang et al. reference must be expanded to the full author list before final publication.
5. A bibliographically verified reference is not automatically evidence of a verified research finding. Study methods, numerical results, limitations and conflicts of interest must be appraised separately.
6. This is a working reference list, not yet the final publication bibliography.
