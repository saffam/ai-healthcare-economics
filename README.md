# The Economics of Artificial Intelligence in Healthcare

## Project Overview

This independent research project examines the economic, institutional and policy implications of artificial intelligence in healthcare.

It investigates whether and under what conditions AI can improve healthcare efficiency, productivity and patient outcomes.

The project also examines human trust, professional judgement, automation bias, organisational differences, economic costs and governance.

## Central Research Question

Under what economic, institutional and policy conditions can artificial intelligence improve healthcare efficiency and outcomes, how should its costs, benefits and risks be evaluated, and to what extent can healthcare professionals, patients and organisations reasonably trust AI-assisted decisions?

## Research Scope

- Economic value and cost-effectiveness
- Healthcare productivity and resource allocation
- Human trust and automation bias
- Healthcare workforce and professional judgement
- Institutional differences and implementation
- Equity, accountability and governance

## Initial Geographical Focus

NHS England, with selected international comparisons.

## Methodology

The project will use a structured literature review, secondary analysis of suitable public and aggregated evidence, and transparent economic modelling.

## Ethics and Data Policy

Only sources with verified access and reuse conditions will be considered.

No identifiable patient records, unauthorised personal data, human recruitment or clinical experimentation will be used.

All sources will be appropriately acknowledged.

## Research Outputs

- Research protocol
- Literature review
- Evidence database
- Economic analysis
- Reproducible Python code
- Academic-style research paper
- Final PDF report
